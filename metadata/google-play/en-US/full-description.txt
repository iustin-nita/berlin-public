Find Berlin public toilets quickly with a simple map built for everyday life in the city. Berlin Public Toilets & More helps you discover essential public amenities across Berlin, including toilets, drinking fountains, EV charging stations, playgrounds, bike repair stations, bathing spots, cool spaces, BBQ areas, and decorative fountains.

Whether you live in Berlin or you are just visiting, search for an address, browse amenity categories, and explore nearby places on the map or in a list.

Use Berlin Public Toilets & More to:

• Find public toilets and drinking fountains
• Locate EV charging stations and bike repair stations
• Discover playgrounds, bathing spots, cool spaces, and BBQ areas
• Browse decorative fountains and other public places
• Filter the map and save favorites for quick access
• Open directions in your preferred navigation app
• Share community reports about whether an amenity is working

After a successful download, cached amenity information remains available with limited connectivity. New map tiles, address searches, refreshed data, and community reports require internet access. This is not a complete offline map.

Location access is optional for browsing. Android address search requires location permission. Community reports are unverified suggestions from other users, and conditions may change.

Public amenity data comes from Berlin's open GIS services. Berlin Public is an independent app and is not an official city service.
